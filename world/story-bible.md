# Story bible

Original characters, places and threads from the Road Tales so far. Canon characters are only listed where these stories give them something new to do.

## Original characters

### Beleriand, First Age

- **Tirnel**: young Sindarin scout on Fingolfin's watch-posts on Ard-galen. Carries the warning of the Dagor Bragollach to Barad Eithel, uncovers the decoy letter, later Fingon's trusted scout. Survives the Nirnaeth Arnoediad and writes down what she saw at the Havens of Círdan. (Story 1)
- **Ulgar**: Easterling who came to Barad Eithel as a refugee and worked as a stable groom. Morgoth's agent. Killed by Gelmir. His dying warning foreshadows the Easterlings' betrayal at the Nirnaeth. (Story 1)
- **Gelmir**: Noldo who "escaped" Angband as a hero, but was secretly bound by Morgoth and passed secrets to Ulgar without knowing. Freed when he learns the truth, and avenges himself. (Story 1)

### The far East, First Age (after the Nirnaeth)

- **Saelon**: old Noldo loremaster, one of the Calaquendi who saw the light of the Trees. Leads the journey east to the Avari. Sings the truth of the Noldor, shame included, and the light of Valinor still in him breaks a sorcerer's binding. (Story 2)
- **Dannil**: Green-elf guide from Ossiriand. (Story 2)
- **Narvi Stonehand**: Dwarf of Belegost, along mainly for the maps. (Story 2)
- **Aerin**: young Man of the House of Bëor, family lost in the Dagor Bragollach. The first Man the Avari have ever seen. (Story 2)
- **Ithrë**: wild Avari scout, clothed in bark and leaves, eyes that shine like a cat's. Leads the Avari archers west with Saelon. (Story 2)
- **Morwë**: one of the oldest Avari lords, by a lake that reminds them of Cuiviénen. (Story 2)
- **Khamûl the Lesser**: Easterling sorcerer-captain, let go with a message for his master. Not the Nazgûl of the same name. (Story 2)

### The East, Second Age

- **Pallando** and **Alatar**: the Blue Wizards (canon names, original story). Sent East to find Sauron's work. Pallando loses much of his strength breaking the second mirror and turns from fighting to gathering peoples. (Stories 3 and 4)
- **Darun**: fisherman's son from a village on the Sea of Rhûn. Helps Pallando escape, finds his brother, becomes Rúmil's student. (Story 3)
- **Tarek**: Darun's older brother, taken by Sauron's priests and freed when the first mirror breaks. (Story 3)
- **Rúmil**: blind Avari Elf, born at Cuiviénen. See his own story below. (Stories 3 and 5)
- **Temur**: herdsman's grandson from the northern grasslands. Twice looked into the mirror and walked away. Becomes Pallando's apprentice and his eyes. (Story 4)
- **Batu**: Temur's grandfather. (Story 4)
- **Kiri**: Hiru huntress, missing two fingers to frostbite, tracked the mirror-priests alone for two winters. Her brother is freed when the second mirror breaks. (Story 4)
- **Borin Ironfist**: Dwarf of the Red Hills, sent by his king after miners kept vanishing into the deep. (Story 4)

### Cuiviénen and the East, before the Sun (Rúmil's story)

- **Rúmil**: hunter, restless, brave. Stays when Oromë leads the host West, to rescue the Elves taken north. Carries Oromë's horn. Taught by Ulmo through the waters. Founds the river-folk. Leads them out of Frár's hall and west into Doriath, where he finds Elenwë.
- **Elenwë**: Rúmil's sister, the singer, gifted with foresight. Set out West with the host but stayed in Beleriand and became a handmaiden of Queen Melian, singing in her choir in Menegroth. Foretold they'd meet again, and kept the carved bird until they did. Gave it back to Rúmil in Menegroth.
- **Tarvo**: Rúmil's childhood friend, rescued from the Black Rider's cage. Goes West.
- **Lindo**: young Elf freed from the first holding-pit. Scout. Loves the stories of the West. First to sing with the Dwarves.
- **Nessa**: older, quiet Elf freed from the pit. Asks the hard questions.
- **Frár**: the first Dwarf the river-folk meet, a smith who sings at his forge. The song of Oromë is sung into his armour and axe. Chooses friendship with the Elves over his own hall and leaves with them, on condition no Dwarf is hurt. Durlin taught him smithing. With his five Dwarves, the first to help delve Menegroth, before the Dwarves of Belegost came. Plans to go to his kin in Belegost and tell them of Melian. Perhaps the later Frár of Moria was named after him.
- **Durlin**: master smith of Frár's hall, jealous of Frár's armour and of the Elves knowing the deep names. Planned to seal the arch at the spring meltwater and keep the Elves, and Frár, in the hall for good.
- **Thrandur**: old keeper of the great doors in Frár's hall. Sided with Durlin, then let the river-folk go because Lindo sang the cracks out of a pillar he'd patched for forty years. Sealed the arch only after they were through.
- **Nali**: young Dwarf who left with Frár. Nearly ran from the visions in the Girdle of Melian. One of five Dwarves who followed Frár.
- **The march-warden**: unnamed Sindarin warden under Beleg, sent by Melian to meet the singers at her border. Had heard rumours of Dwarves in the eastern hills but never seen one, since this is before any Dwarves had come to Doriath.

## Places

- **Barad Eithel**: Fingolfin's fortress at Eithel Sirion. The hidden western postern, the eastern wall attack.
- **The Avari lake**: a lake far east of the Blue Mountains that reminds the Avari of Cuiviénen. Morwë's people.
- **Darun's village**: on the shore of the Sea of Rhûn. Later known for the Star-folk, who can't be bought by Sauron's priests.
- **The fortress in the hills**: southeast of the Sea of Rhûn, home of the first mirror. A fallen Maia kept it. Seven mirrors were marked on the map found there.
- **The Hiru coast**: snow-houses of the Hiru, seal hunters of the far north.
- **The lake that doesn't freeze**: steaming black lake in the northern ice, with the second mirror in a black glass tower on an island. Ancient dwarf tunnels run under it.
- **The Red Hills**: Borin's people's hold, east of the ice.
- **The ravine of thorns**: north of Cuiviénen, where Rúmil and Elenwë first drove back the Black Rider with song.
- **The cave of the false song**: at the foot of the northern mountains, where a servant of Melkor sang captives into sitting still.
- **Frár's hall**: a Dwarf-hold where these Dwarves woke, reached by a river that vanishes into the mountainside. Its arch is now sealed by a great stone.
- **The western pass**: a narrow pass over the mountains, so tight the Dwarves had to turn sideways, that Ulmo's water led the river-folk through into Beleriand.
- **The Girdle of Melian**: the river-folk sang their way in with a song of searching and finding, longing and friendship. The wood showed each of them their fears, then the hope beneath.
- **Menegroth**: in these stories, early Menegroth, before the Dwarves of Belegost. Only the first caves exist, part rough stone. Frár's Dwarves begin the delving, with Elvish singers shaping the stone into beech trees and birds. Where Rúmil found Elenwë in a choir in the lower caves.

## Things that exist

- **The mirrors**: seven great mirrors of black glass made for Sauron in the East. Whoever looks in is bound. Two broken so far (the hills, and the frozen lake). Five left, including one in the southern deserts, one in an eastern island sea, and three in places with unknown names.
- **Oromë's horn**: small antler horn given to Rúmil. Its call puts Melkor's creatures to flight. Now cracked, and Ulmo speaks through the crack as running water.
- **The blue crystals**: the Blue Wizards leave them at waypoints to send messages.
- **Pallando's staff**: cracked breaking the second mirror, slowly mending.
- **Frár's armour and axe**: sung with the song of Oromë. The mail ripples like northern lights; the axe sings like a horn.

## Open threads

- **The Blue Wizards' pact**: gather allies from every people and meet at Darun's village on the Sea of Rhûn in a year. Alatar went south to the Sun-King, who serves Sauron for a promise of eternal life, with the desert raider Hazad, the Sun-King's former general.
- **Rúmil's road**: the player's plan is now played through. The river-folk escaped Frár's hall with Frár and five Dwarves, never found Angband, crossed into Beleriand and reached Menegroth, where Rúmil found Elenwë. Thingol welcomed them and set Frár's Dwarves to work on the halls. This part of the story ends quietly in Doriath.
- **Continuity to settle**: in Story 3, Rúmil is blind and living alone in the East in the Second Age, and says his sister "went south to the lakes when I was blinded". Rúmil's own story sends him to Beleriand to find her in Menegroth. Still to work out: how he is blinded, how he ends up back in the East, and what that line about his sister means.
- **Melian's warning**: hearing of Durlin's jealousy, Melian foresaw that a treasure brighter than anything Frár could make would one day come into Menegroth, and Dwarves and Elves would both desire it, at great cost to the kingdom. Foreshadows the Silmaril, the Nauglamír and the sack of Menegroth (canon). Nobody understood it at the time and it was forgotten.
- **Frár and Belegost**: Frár's Dwarves were the first to help build Menegroth. When Frár reaches Belegost and tells of Melian, that could be how the Dwarves of Belegost come to Doriath in canon.
- **The Easterlings' warning**: Ulgar's people crossing the mountains, leading to Ulfang's betrayal at the Nirnaeth (canon).
